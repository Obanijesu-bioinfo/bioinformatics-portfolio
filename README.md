## Undergraduate Research

**Comparative Evaluation of the In Vitro Anti-Inflammatory Potential of *Helianthus annuus* and *Ixora coccinea* Floret Extracts**

My undergraduate research investigated the in vitro anti-inflammatory potential of sunflower (*Helianthus annuus*) and *Ixora coccinea* floret extracts.

Practical research activities included:

- plant material preparation and methanolic extraction;
- spectrophotometric measurements;
- a protein denaturation inhibition assay;
- a proteinase inhibition assay using trypsin;
- biochemical and anti-nutritional-factor analysis;
- concentration-dependent experimental investigations.

Experimental data were analysed using GraphPad Prism 9, including IC50 estimation and statistical comparisons using ANOVA and Tukey's multiple-comparison test.

## Featured Computational Research

### RNA-seq Analysis of GSE213410: 5-OXO-ETE and Calcitriol

**Project type:** Educational reanalysis of publicly available transcriptomic data<br>
**Dataset:** NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO), [GSE213410](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE213410)

This project investigates gene-expression patterns in two separate experimental comparisons involving human stem-cell models.

The workflow covers:

- dataset exploration and quality assessment;
- sample metadata organisation;
- transcript-to-gene aggregation;
- differential gene-expression analysis;
- functional enrichment analysis;
- pathway investigation;
- biological interpretation and annotation review;
- reproducibility checks and documentation.

**Tools:** R, DESeq2, clusterProfiler, ReactomePA, fgsea, Python, pandas, Matplotlib and Git.

The analysis examines methodological limitations, sample quality concerns and the interpretation of uncertain findings. The C1 comparison remains unresolved. The exploratory analysis excluding sample A3 is a sensitivity analysis, not definitive evidence.

The project uses transcript-level count estimates supplied with the GEO dataset. Raw sequencing reads were not independently aligned or quantified.
